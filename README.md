# Telecom AI Research

## Overview
This repository documents my learning and research activities in
Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and
Telecommunication Networks.

The main research interest is AI-assisted energy optimization
for cellular base stations, with a focus on traffic-aware
power consumption modeling and sustainable next-generation
telecommunication networks.

## Research Objectives
- Study the relationship between network traffic and energy consumption.
- Explore Machine Learning models for predicting base-station power usage.
- Investigate energy-efficient strategies for 5G and emerging 6G networks.
- Analyze renewable energy integration in telecom infrastructure.
- Develop a data-driven research project with reproducible experiments.

## Research Areas
- AI and Machine Learning for Telecom
- Traffic-Aware Energy Profiling
- Base Station Power Consumption Prediction
- Green Communications and Renewable Energy
- 5G/6G Network Energy Optimization
